01 / THE MISSION

A place to go
beyond theory.

C2P is a space for the people who refuse to leave their best ideas on paper.

Rooted in the Computer Engineering community at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, this independent site concept showcases a culture of building, learning through experimentation and sharing knowledge.

This is an independently designed website prototype; its programs, membership processes and institutional affiliation require confirmation before public launch.
